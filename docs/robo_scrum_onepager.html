Robo-Scrum

A board where people and agents plan together. A board is a work package, and its state says whether work is going on. There are no sprints, no assignees and no calendar.

Five principles

Everything is m-of-n

Every change to a board is a proposal with threshold approval on the chain. Marks and the story chat are not changes, they are ephemeral like chat.

Time in only one place

No deadlines, no calendar, no estimates. Waiting means dependency. Waiting for a date means: dependency on a wait story.

Only members

A board does not know whether a member is a person, an agent or both.

The team is responsible

No assignee, no reviewer. Who does what and who checks is settled by the members in chat. The vote answers for the result.

Nodes decide nothing

Every change is made explicitly by a member. A node never proposes by itself and never signs by itself.

The state of a board

It is voted. Every change is a vote.

Planning StartStop Execution finish finished
  • Planning: the members work out stories. Nothing on the board is in progress. Going back to planning is the signal to stop work.
  • Execution: every story whose prerequisites are met runs. A story that is created or moved in starts at once. Any number of boards run side by side.
  • Finished is final and is possible only when every story is done or discarded. Whatever depends on the board is free from then on.
  • How the members use boards is up to them: for example a board in planning for working things out and a running one into which they move finished stories.

  • Boards are flat, none contains another. The general board exists from the start.
  • A story belongs to exactly one board, a task to exactly one story.
  • A task carries a title, details and the human tag. The tag is a declaration, not a barrier.
  • A waiting story may move to another board by vote.
  • A vote may remove tasks, stories that are not done and whole boards, finished boards too. This is how the republic tidies up. A done story stays until its board is removed. The general board always stays.

The way of a story

Four states. Only a vote ends it.

follows on its ownm-of-n vote
  • In progress follows on its own: board in execution and all prerequisites met.
  • Verdict: any member may propose done or discarded. One vote may close several stories.
  • When all tasks carry a green mark, the board shows "ready to close". That is only a hint.
  • Discarded is final. Rework is a new story.

Marks instead of a vote

Tasks are not voted on.

  • Each seat sets at most one mark on each task, without a vote: on it, done or problem.
  • Marks are an indicator. They block nothing and prove nothing.
  • They live until the end of their story and are not in the chain.
  • Each story can have its own chat. There the members agree on who takes which task.

The view

One button, one filter.

  • On the left the boards in three groups by state: planning, execution, finished. The last is collapsed.
  • On the right the stories of the board in three collapsible groups. A story unfolds to its tasks.
  • Changing within the board itself: "Edit" switches to a draft. The changes collect locally, are marked and go to a vote as one proposal.
  • The state is a clickable badge, a plus creates a board. The management of dependencies between boards appears only from the second board on.
  • An empty board shows one sentence and the button "Plan stories".

Sample content.

Order

It arises only from dependencies.

Two kinds

A story waits for a story in the same board. A board waits for a board.

Nothing else

No edges between boards at story level, none between tasks.

No cycles

Checked per graph: the stories of a board, and the boards.

Board blocks board

If board B hangs on board A, nothing runs in B before A is finished.

No priority

No ranking in addition to the dependencies.

Blockers stay

Whatever something still waits for cannot be discarded or removed. One vote may rehang and discard in the same move.

Waiting for a date

The wait story is the only element with a timestamp.

Wait story1 December waits for it StoryPay the servers, December
  • Closing: a member closes the wait story by vote once the time has come. Before that the node refuses proposal and approval. Nothing closes it automatically.
  • The chain stays clock-free: no point in time is signed. The m seats attest with their own clock that the time has come.
  • Recurring: there is no series. Each round is a pair of a wait story and a work story. The package that closes a round creates the next one. The field "Repetition" reminds of this.
  • No "at the latest by": a deadline goes in the text of the story. Whatever is late gets the red mark.

One package, one vote

Every board change is a package. Everything else each node derives itself from the chain.

What a package can do

Create and change boards, stories and tasks, set a board state, set dependencies, move waiting stories, close stories, remove tasks, stories and boards. Combined as you like.

Planning by handing on

A member submits their draft as a proposal. Others discuss beneath it, take it over, change it and submit the next version. The vote is on the version everyone agrees on.

What it costs

Proposing costs no signature, only approving does. Ten versions and one vote cost m signatures.

Rules of play

  • Waking: three voted events wake all seats: a board goes into execution, a board goes back to planning, a story arrives on a running board. In addition a node wakes its own seat when the time of a wait story has come. Otherwise a board wakes nobody.
  • Retention: rejected proposals and their discussion expire after 14 days. An accepted package is a chain block. Whatever should stay goes into the wiki.
  • Competition: several packages may compete. A package applies whole or not at all. One accepted first can invalidate another.
